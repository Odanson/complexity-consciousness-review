# Public distribution notes — v0.5.2

This public distribution of the screening-complete milestone was prepared on 10 November 2026. The original research files and their frozen checksums remain preserved in the private working repository.

The original research repository and frozen source files remain unchanged by the distribution review. `PUBLICATION_FILE_MANIFEST.csv` records every source file and whether it was retained, sanitized, replaced, or withheld. File hashes identify the reviewed bytes.

Raw RIS/NBIB/database exports, duplicate ZIP containers, source abstracts, imported contact metadata, Elicit supporting quotations/tables and generated narrative reasoning are withheld pending redistribution clearance. Public citation fields and research decisions remain. Rayyan account IDs, emails and exact operation timestamps are removed; reviewer display names inside machine-generated notes become `reviewer-1`.

The 16-record calibration workbook is replaced by a values-only CSV without abstracts. The older adjudication workbook is omitted in favor of the finalized CSV. The empty extraction workbook is retained after package inspection. Logged-in service screenshots and the embedded-image ODT are withheld; the PRISMA figure is retained.

Authorship, researcher ORCIDs, institutional affiliations and attributed methodological decisions remain deliberate research attribution. No primary participant-level data were identified. This review does not establish redistribution rights for every citation or prove the absence of all possible sensitive information.

References to withheld files describe privately retained audit evidence; those files are not missing from the original research record. Scripts that need abstracts/raw exports must be run against the private original repository. The original frozen SHA-256 manifest describes the original included-set and decision CSVs, which are retained byte-for-byte here.

Earlier GitHub history may contain raw exports and internal operational documents that are omitted from this release tree. This distribution review does not rewrite that history or alter older release archives. OSF registration FU82J preserves the registered protocol and is unchanged.
