# Empirical-seed gate — supervisor review (2026-09-10)

**Reviewed by:** Prof Jurgen Jost, Prof Lucia Melloni, Dr Andrej Bicanski (supervisors)
**Date:** 2026-09-10

Two of the eight registered empirical seed papers (calibration_seeds.ris) are human-Excluded from the final corpus. Below is the supervisor's rationale for each.

---

## Seed: Carhart-Harris et al. 2014 — The entropic brain

**Title:** The entropic brain: a theory of conscious states informed by neuroimaging research

**Rayyan article_id:** 570553226 (part 1)

**Human decision:** Exclude

**Elicit verdict:** Exclude

**Supervisor rationale:**

> This paper is excluded because it is a theoretical synthesis. It centrally addresses conscious states and discusses eligible measures—entropy, criticality, and functional-connectivity repertoires—but does not newly calculate them from an original empirical dataset. It therefore fails the empirical-data requirement (§B4 exclude #1: "No neural data; pure theory, or simulation-only without a stated empirical extension").

**Decision:** Remains excluded. No appeal. The paper remains a valuable citation for the consciousness-theory background, but does not meet the corpus inclusion criterion requiring original empirical neural data.

---

## Seed: Luppi et al. 2022 — A synergistic core

**Title:** A synergistic core for human brain evolution and cognition

**Rayyan article_id:** 570554945 (part 1)

**Human decision:** Exclude

**Elicit verdict:** No verdict (coverage gap)

**Supervisor rationale:**

> This paper is excluded because its empirical target is cognitive architecture and human brain evolution, not consciousness. It uses eligible information-decomposition measures, including synergy and redundancy, but does not compare, manipulate, or assess conscious states, contents, or levels. It therefore fails the consciousness-focus requirement (§B4 exclude #2: "Consciousness is mentioned but not measured or manipulated").

**Decision:** Remains excluded. No appeal. The paper is an important cognitive-neuroscience reference but is outside the scope of this consciousness-complexity review.
